Privacy Policy

Data transparency for users and creators.

Image Processing & Security

All images processed through Veloce Studio are handled locally inside your web browser. We never upload, transmit, or archive your source files or cutouts on any external cloud server.

History Vault

Your recent cutouts are stored exclusively within your browser's local storage so you can easily access them during your session. You can clear them at any time using the trash button in the sidebar.

Advertising & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google and its third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites across the internet.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
